# The One-Winner Playbook — farming a confirmed winner

*Companion to THE_STRATEGY.md · drafted 2026-07-20 · worked example: MU.*
*A winner is an asset with multiple harvestable streams. The streams are
not equal. Ranked by contribution — and by what they must never damage.*

---

## Layer 0 — The compounding engine (90%+ of everything; costs nothing)

**The highest-yield action on a winner is refusing to interfere.**
No exits, no trimming, no profit-taking, no covered calls — ever, on the
conviction core. The return arrives in violent bursts you cannot time;
every tested interference gave up more than it saved. Layer 0 is a
discipline, not an activity. Every layer below is permitted only if it
cannot damage this one.

- Status check, weekly, automatic: the tracker door. IN-TREND = farm
  normally. BROKEN = reviews begin (thesis reread, narrative check);
  the farm's aggressive layers pause. 4 consecutive BROKEN weeks =
  escalated review per the book's replacement rule.
- The "winner" label is provisional — earned weekly, never granted.

## Layer 1 — Pyramiding (growing the engine)

- ADD whenever capital allows while the trend is intact. No pattern-
  waiting — tested: base-resolution timing is worse than just adding.
- Feed the position from OUTSIDE cash flow and from Layer 2's income —
  two compounding sources.
- Set an ADDS ceiling (a % of net worth beyond which you stop feeding
  it). Never cap what it grows INTO by appreciation. Limit the fuel,
  never the fire.

## Layer 2 — Put-side income (the dividend a growth stock doesn't pay)

The winner's high IV is an asset. Sell it WITHOUT touching the upside:

- **Bull put spread** (or CSP if you want assignment): short strike
  ~10% below spot, ~5% wide, ~40 DTE.
- Close at 60% of credit. Close on short-strike breach. Never hope.
- Gates (all mandatory): trend intact · weekly structure not Downtrend
  · NOT parabolic (exhaustion TOP flags ≥2 = stand down) · no earnings
  within 14 days · IV−RV positive (premium actually rich).
- Assignment is a feature: buying more of your winner ~10% cheaper is
  Layer 1, pre-priced. Size so assignment is always WELCOME — that is
  the sizing rule.
- MU worked example (2026-07-19 live quotes): 765/720 spread, Aug 28,
  $15.78 credit vs $29.22 max loss.
- **Evidence (overlay backtest, 160 names, 10y):** on ≥4x WINNERS
  (n=71), systematic spreads were net-positive after all correction
  losses on **91.5% of names**, 76% cycle win rate, median worst YEAR
  only −$235 per contract — the overlay never threatened the core.
  Median yield **2.3%/yr at conservative model pricing** (BS without
  skew, credits ~half of live-chain quotes like MU's; no earnings
  blackout modeled, cutting the other way). Honest expectation:
  **mid-single-digit %/yr** — real, safe, and small next to the
  engine: overlay income was just 6% of the winners' hold P&L.
  The dimes are real dimes. The engine is still the business.

## Layer 3 — Harvesting the winner's own corrections

**Every great winner is periodically a VBT-1 candidate.** NVDA fell 30%+
five times on the way to 45x. The machinery already runs:

- Deep correction begins → stop Layer 2 when structure breaks (gate
  does this automatically). Do not sell the core (exit study).
- CAPITULATIVE character flag (bottom-decile extension) → the best-paid
  moment for cash-secured puts on a name you know: paid knife-catching,
  worst case = more of your winner near the low. (hpat study: 5-day
  bounce asymmetry ~2:1 at capitulation.)
- Confirmed BOTTOM (stage-3, the 76%-win signal) → the aggressive
  re-add. This is a scheduled harvest event, not an emergency.
- Narrative check: gloom at the signal is NORMAL (62% of winning events).
  Fear in the headlines while structure confirms is the majority case of
  a working signal — never wait for the narrative to improve, and never
  read improvement as confirmation. (Full study 2026-08-01: tone adds no
  information on quality names; only sunny-narrative SPEC signals warrant
  caution.)

## Layer 4 — Convexity & capital efficiency (advanced shelf; paper first)

- **LEAPS stock replacement**: deep-ITM calls (~0.80 delta, 12-24mo)
  hold ~80% of exposure for ~half the capital; max loss = premium.
  The one respectable leverage. Costs: theta, IV, no dividend.
  UNTESTED in our lab — paper and journal before any size.
- **Risk reversal** (sell put below, credit buys call above): the
  parabolic-phase expression — exploits rich put skew + the tested
  finding that parabolas continue. Adds downside share-equivalent
  exposure: only at sizes where assignment is welcome. UNTESTED.
- **Margin: never.** The only leverage that can force a sale at the
  exact bottom — converts survivable MAE into permanent loss.

## Layer 5 — Concentrated-position insurance (a sleep decision, not yield)

When the position becomes a life-changing fraction of net worth:
the **collar** (zero-cost: ~5% OTM put financed by a call) is the only
tested device that genuinely cuts single-name drawdowns (−57%→−48%),
at ~20% of expectancy. Use it for sleep, on size that matters, in
BROKEN regimes — and know its price. (4d study.)

## The two laws of the farm

1. **The dimes never endanger the engine.** Layers 2–4 might add
   10–20%/yr. Layer 0 is where 10x lives. Any structure that could
   force a core sale, cap the tail, or complicate a correction is
   banned regardless of yield.
2. **Conditionality.** The whole stack runs on a confirmed trend and a
   living thesis. Doors and flags decide which layers are open this
   week — the tracker page is the farm report.

*Every layer above traces to a study in THESIS_PLAN.md. The untested
shelf (Layer 4) earns its way in through the journal like everything
else did.*
