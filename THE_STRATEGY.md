# The Strategy — read this when in doubt

*One page. Every line was earned by a test, not an opinion.*
*(VBT, 2026-07-19. Evidence in THESIS_PLAN.md and RULEBOOK.md.)*

## The one idea

**You cannot predict the future. You don't need to.**
The money comes from three asymmetries that pay without foresight,
collected with discipline, for years:

1. **Selection** — own quality names inside secular themes. The same
   entry signal that merely market-performs on a random stock earns
   +12% median excess on a well-chosen one. The edge is in WHAT you
   own, not when you click.
2. **The fat tail** — mean returns are 2–3× medians. One or two
   holdings will make the entire decade. You don't know which. So you
   never cap, never trim early, never sell the winner. Protecting the
   right tail IS the strategy.
3. **Time horizon** — funds get redeemed at −22% drawdowns; you don't.
   Sitting through pain the crowd structurally cannot sit through is
   the only edge institutions can't copy. Patience is the moat.

## The rules (each survived a backtest; most alternatives didn't)

- **ENTER** through two doors only: the 13/52-week MA cross (the calm
  standing gate) or a confirmed deep-correction bottom (the aggressive
  door — 76% win, +25% median historically). Never chase 52-week-high
  breakouts: they lose to just holding the ETF.
- **HOLD without exit rules.** Every tested exit destroyed wealth
  (best case kept 82 cents of the hold dollar). A broken trend
  triggers a REVIEW — reread the thesis — never an automatic sale.
  Drawdown control comes from position sizing, not from selling.
- **ADD whenever capital allows** inside an intact trend. Waiting for
  pullback patterns is worse than adding on any random week — tested.
- **SIZE so no single outcome can end you**: 3–4 conviction names,
  max 2 per theme, speculative names at half weight, options risk
  capped at a fixed sleeve. Survival first; the expectancy needs years
  to compound.
- **OPTIONS: sell the side you don't want, keep the side you do.**
  Income = put spreads below names you'd happily own more of. Never
  covered calls on conviction holdings (that sells the fat tail).
  Defined risk always. No premium selling within 14 days of earnings
  or when IV is cheap. In parabolic runs: stop selling premium; never
  buy puts to call the top — parabolas outlast every top-caller
  (tested: +1.6% median 3-day drift AFTER the "top" pattern).
- **Timeframes know their place**: weekly decides regime, daily
  colors character (normal / parabolic / capitulative), hourly is for
  execution prices only. Five studies in a row: timing skill dies as
  the timeframe shrinks.
- **Exhaustion grades risk, never triggers entries.** Euphoria says
  stop selling puts; capitulation says the bottom-hunt is near. The
  confirmed weekly signal still pulls every trigger.

## The discipline that makes it work

- Every judgment is written, dated, and scored later. What isn't
  logged doesn't count. What can't be falsified isn't believed.
- Paper record first, size never before the record earns it. The
  system's job is to prove itself; yours is to not interfere.
- When a rule and a feeling disagree, the rule wins — the rule was
  tested on hundreds of cases; the feeling on one.

## When you feel the urge to act

A drawdown is not a signal. A hot streak is not a signal. The urge to
"do something" is the most expensive signal of all. The tested moves
are: buy at open doors, add in intact trends, review on breaks, sell
puts when paid well — and otherwise, deliberately, nothing.

**Expectancy × survival × time. That's the whole game.**
